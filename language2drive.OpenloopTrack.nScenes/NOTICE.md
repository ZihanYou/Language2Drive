The same attribution, non-commercial and share-alike terms apply to the derived replay bundle; see its `assets/DATA_LICENSE.md`.

Please credit Holger Caesar et al., *nuScenes: A multimodal dataset for autonomous driving*, CVPR 2020, and the [nuScenes project](https://www.nuscenes.org/). Original map files are unchanged. Benchmark instructions, contracts and prediction records are additional evaluation artifacts. Camera images, LiDAR scans, upstream model implementations and weights are not included in the replay bundle. Obtain those assets from their respective providers under their own terms.
